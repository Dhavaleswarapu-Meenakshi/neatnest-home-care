Our Offerings

Carefully Differentiated Cleaning Services

Every household has different cleaning priorities. We offer structured service options tailored to regular upkeep, seasonal deep cleans, or property transitions.

Recurring Maintenance

Regular Home Cleaning

Consistent housekeeping to keep your home continuously fresh and welcoming. Ideal for ongoing maintenance on weekly, fortnightly, or monthly visits.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Dusting all reachable surfaces, shelves, and tabletops
  • Vacuuming carpets, rugs, and mopping hard floors
  • Bathroom sanitization, mirrors, and basin polishing
  • Kitchen countertops, cooktop wipe-down, and sink care
  • Emptying household bins and neat linen straightening
Comprehensive Reset

Deep Cleaning Service

An intensive, top-to-bottom reset targeting built-up dust, grease, grime, and neglected corners that regular daily cleaning does not reach.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Detailed wiping of baseboards, door frames, and switches
  • Interior window sills, tracks, and glass wipe-down
  • Deep descaling of bathroom tile grout, screens, and taps
  • Degreasing range hoods, exterior cabinetry, and splashbacks
  • Behind and beneath reachable lightweight furniture
Transition Ready

Move-In / Move-Out Cleaning

Specialized cleaning designed for vacant properties before unpacking or handing keys over. Ensures spaces are immaculately prepared for new occupants.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Complete interior cleaning of empty cupboards and drawers
  • Sanitizing all wardrobe shelving and closet interiors
  • Wall spot-cleaning and scuff removal where appropriate
  • Comprehensive deep sanitization of all bathrooms
  • Full vacuuming, edging, and mopping of bare floors
High-Traffic Focus

Kitchen & Bathroom Detail

Targeted hygiene and descaling for the most demanding moisture and food preparation spaces in the home, restoring sparkle and cleanliness.

Key Focus Areas:

  • Oven door exterior, stovetop grates, and extractor filter hoods
  • Microwave interior and exterior appliance shine
  • Limescale and soap scum removal from shower screens
  • High-grade sanitization of sinks, faucets, and toilets
  • Tile splashbacks and grout line detailed scrubbing

Need a specialized routine or partial cleaning? Custom room checklists are arranged directly during your quote consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions regarding service scope, home access, supplies, and how we coordinate with homeowners.

Do you bring your own cleaning supplies and equipment? +

In a typical arrangement, standard cleaning equipment, microfiber cloths, and general detergents are provided. If your home features specialized finishes (such as unsealed natural marble, vintage hardwood, or specialty fixtures) or you prefer specific eco-certified brands, we gladly adapt our routine to your designated products.

Do I need to be at home during the cleaning visit? +

That is entirely up to your personal comfort. Many clients prefer being present during the first visit to walk through specific focus areas and preferences. For ongoing routines, key lockboxes, concierge access, or scheduled entry windows can be arranged securely.

How are quotes and estimates determined? +

Estimates are based on the square footage, number of bedrooms and bathrooms, general property condition, and whether you require regular upkeep or an in-depth restorative clean. This ensures you only pay for what your home actually requires rather than generic flat rates.

Are services pet-friendly? +

Yes. We love pets and understand that cats and dogs are part of the family. During our initial consultation, we note any pet temperaments, doors that must remain shut, and preferences regarding vacuum noise or pet-safe cleaning agents.

Can I customize the rooms or tasks for each visit? +

Yes. Our priority checklists are completely flexible. If you would like us to dedicate extra time to deep scrubbing the kitchen oven one week and focus on guest bedrooms the next, simply let us know in advance of your visit.
